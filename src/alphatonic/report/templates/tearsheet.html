Time-series evaluation · prediction within each asset

Full-period IC correlates scores and forward returns over time for each asset. Monthly TS ICIR is mean / sample standard deviation of complete calendar-month ICs. It requires {{ ts_options.min_months }} valid months and is marked preliminary below twelve.

Confidence intervals use circular blocks of paired observations, sampled together across assets. Asset quartiles describe dispersion, not uncertainty. The universe monthly ratio is computed after averaging eligible asset ICs each month; changing asset coverage can affect it. No ICIR significance test is implied.
